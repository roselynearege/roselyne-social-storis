AN AFRICAN FAMILY STORY

I AM AN ORPHAN, NOT YOUR SLAVE!

"You have no parents to defend you!" My stepmother shouted.

The little girl wiped her tears, looked into her eyes and replied:

"I may have lost my parents, but I have not lost my dignity. I am an orphan, not your slave. I deserve love, not humiliation."

For the first time, the woman had nothing to say.

MY STORY DOES NOT DEFINE MY WORTH.

❤️ I deserve respect. I deserve love.

COURAGE • CONFIDENCE • SELF-RESPECT
